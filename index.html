Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of embroidery services do you offer?

We offer a complete range of hand embroidery services including Bridal Aari Work, Simple Border Designs, Kids ethnic embroidery, Zardosi work, Traditional Maggam frames, Kundan beadwork, and custom-styled patterns.

What is the pricing model for custom Aari designs?

Pricing depends on the complexity of the design, coverage area, and materials used (e.g. gold thread, Kundan stones, pearls). Simple necklines start around ₹800, while heavy bridal blouses range from ₹3,500 to ₹10,000+.

What is the typical delivery timeframe?

Simple designs are completed in 3-5 days. Heavy bridal and Zardosi blouse orders take about 7-14 days. We also offer express bridal delivery (under 4 days) on special requests for wedding emergencies.

Can I provide my own blouse fabric and custom design?

Absolutely! You can bring your own fabric material and share design references (photos, sketches). Our designer will consult with you on color combinations and match it perfectly with your saree.
