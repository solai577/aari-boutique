Our Legacy

The Heritage of Aari Embroidery

Originating centuries ago, Aari embroidery is the ultimate art of fine detailing. The needle, shaped like a fine crochet hook, is used to stitch chains of gold thread, beads, and silk. At Aari Work Boutique in Kamuthi, we preserve this fine traditional art form while injecting modern, trendy patterns tailored for the contemporary Indian woman.

Every single blouse we create goes through a meticulous design process: from selecting premium materials, drafting patterns on tracing sheets, setting up the wooden Maggam frames, to hand-stitching each element stone-by-stone.

How We Work

Our Embroidery Process

01

Design Consultation

We discuss your saree color, event type, and pattern preferences. You can choose from our archives or bring your own reference photo.

02

Tracing & Setup

The design pattern is precisely traced on paper and transferred onto the fabric. The fabric is then stretched taut on our wooden Maggam frame.

03

Hand Embroidery

Our skilled artisans manually stitch the patterns using gold Zari cords, beads, Kundan stones, and colored silk threads, matching it perfectly.

04

Quality & Delivery

We inspect the embroidery finish, secure loose threads, perform stitching alignments, and deliver a pristine, ironed garment right on schedule.
